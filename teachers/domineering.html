Domineering

▸ How to Play

The Setup

Two players take turns placing dominoes on a grid. Each domino covers exactly two adjacent squares.

  • Horizontal places dominoes left-to-right, covering two squares in the same row.
  • Vertical places dominoes top-to-bottom, covering two squares in the same column.

This app is designed for either two human players alternating turns or one human playing both Horizontal and Vertical.

Click any empty square to place your domino. The preview highlights where it will land. Start with a 2×2 or 2×3 board to get the feel, then work up to larger boards.

Winning

The player who cannot move on their turn loses. There are no draws.

  • Horizontal loses when no two horizontally adjacent empty squares remain.
  • Vertical loses when no two vertically adjacent empty squares remain.
💡 Play & Explore: Try changing the board dimensions as well as "First move" and replay to see who how each effects the strategies available to the two players.
