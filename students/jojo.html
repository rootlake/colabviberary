System Defense Protocol

  • SCANNING Double-Tap any moving virus to scan its health, speed, and special ability cooldowns.
  • DATA LINK CABLE The blue cable at the top connects your server. If Sharks (🦈) bite it or Ransomware (🔒) encrypts it, rapidly click/tap it to repair it before you lose Integrity!
